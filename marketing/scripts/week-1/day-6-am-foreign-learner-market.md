# Day 6 — AM — Foreign Learner — Bargaining at the market

**Archetype:** Foreign learner
**Length:** 25–30s
**Setting:** Pasar / Ubud market or souvenir stall. Mid-morning.
**Caption:** I tried bargaining at a Bali market in Bahasa for the first time
🛍️ #bali #pasar #bahasa

---

## Hook (0–3s)
*(walking into a market, holding up a sarong)*

> "Day 6. I'm trying to bargain at a Bali market in Indonesian — and not
> embarrass myself."

**On-screen text:** *Day 6: market run, in Bahasa.*

## Setup (3–8s)
*(holding up a price tag, doing comedic *yikes* face)*

> "First price is always the foreigner price. The fix isn't pushing — it's
> one polite phrase."

## Phrase (8–16s)
*(direct to camera)*

> "*Boleh kurang?* — *can it be less?* That's the whole bargain. Smile.
> Don't push."

**On-screen text:** *boleh kurang? = can it be cheaper?*

## Situation (16–25s)
*(turns to the seller, holds up the sarong)*

> "*Berapa?* — how much? *Boleh kurang?* — can it be less?"
>
> *(seller smiles, comes down on price)*
>
> "Got 30% off. Said *terima kasih, kak.* Walked out a tiny bit smug."

## CTA (25–30s)
> "I'm drilling this stuff at speak-indo.com — 5 minutes a day, free, link
> in bio."

**End card:** *Practice free → speak-indo.com*
