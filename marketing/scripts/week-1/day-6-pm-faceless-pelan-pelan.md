# Day 6 — PM — Faceless — *pelan-pelan, kak*

**Archetype:** Faceless Bali survival guide
**Length:** 18–22s
**Format:** AI VO + Bali street b-roll + bold phrase + app screen rec end.
**Caption:** save this for when locals speak too fast in Bali 🗣️ #bali #bahasa
#travel

---

## Hook (0–3s)
**B-roll:** quick cuts of Bali street chatter, busy warung, market noise.
**VO:** *"Save this for when locals speak too fast and you panic."*
**On-screen text:** *Locals speaking too fast?*

## Situation (3–8s)
**B-roll:** foreigner-POV at a counter, person across speaking quickly,
*overwhelmed* expression.
**VO:** *"You don't need to give up and switch to English. You need one
phrase."*

## Phrase (8–14s)
**B-roll:** zoom on lips, calm.
**VO:** *"Pelan-pelan, kak. Slow down, friend. Polite, soft, locals love
it."*
**On-screen text fills the frame:**
- *pelan-pelan = slowly*
- *kak = friend / older sibling*
- ***pelan-pelan, kak***

## Practice the sound (14–17s)
**VO:** *"Say it with me. Pe-lan pe-lan, kak. Pelan-pelan, kak."*

## CTA (17–22s)
**B-roll:** screen rec — Speak Indo app, user records the phrase, AI gives
green checkmark, score appears.
**VO:** *"Drill it free at speak-indo.com."*
**End card:** *Practice free → speak-indo.com*
