**Phrase progression:** the week is sequenced so a viewer who finds us on Day 1
and follows along learns a coherent starter set: *terima kasih kak → jangan
pedas → saya di depan → sama-sama → ordering food → villa check-in → boleh
kurang → pelan-pelan → AI roleplay → 3 wrap-up phrases.* That's a real Bali
survival kit by Sunday.

## Production notes

- Run-time target: **20–35 seconds**.
- Vertical 9:16, captions burnt in.
- Show the **phrase as on-screen text** the moment it's spoken.
- End every video with the CTA card: *"Practice this free at speak-indo.com"*.
- Foreign learner videos: shoot 3–5 takes per script, pick the most natural.
- Indonesian coach videos: shoot in one take, prioritize warmth over polish.
- Faceless videos: AI VO + Bali b-roll + on-screen phrase + app screen
  recording at the end.
