# Day 3 — AM — Foreign Learner — Gojek call POV

**Archetype:** Foreign learner
**Length:** 25–30s
**Setting:** Outside a villa or hotel lobby, phone in hand, scooter helmet
under arm.
**Caption:** the Gojek driver always calls. here's the one phrase that saves
me every time 📞 #bali #gojek #bahasa

---

## Hook (0–3s)
*(holding the phone up, slightly stressed)*

> "Every Gojek driver in Bali calls you. And every time, I freeze."

**On-screen text:** *POV: Gojek driver calling.*

## Story (3–10s)
*(self-deprecating laugh)*

> "Last week I tried *English English English* into the phone — and got
> abandoned in a parking lot. Not doing that again."

## Phrase (10–18s)
*(mimes picking up the phone, calm)*

> "Now I just say one thing: *halo, pak — saya di depan.* Hello sir, I'm
> out front. That's it. That's the whole script."

**On-screen text:**
- *halo, pak = hello, sir*
- *saya di depan = I'm out front*

## Situation (18–25s)
*(b-roll: actual phone call mimic, then driver pulling up, thumbs up
exchange)*

> "Driver pulls up in 30 seconds. No more parking-lot abandonment."

## CTA (25–30s)
> "I'm practicing the AI roleplay version at speak-indo.com — link in bio.
> Five minutes a day, free."

**End card:** *Practice free → speak-indo.com*
