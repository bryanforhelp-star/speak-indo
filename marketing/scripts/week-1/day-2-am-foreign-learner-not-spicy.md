# Day 2 — AM — Foreign Learner — *jangan pedas* (POV)

**Archetype:** Foreign learner
**Length:** 25–30s
**Setting:** Sitting at a warung, plate of nasi goreng in front, scooter
helmet on the chair. POV-style.
**Caption:** I tried ordering "no spicy" in Bahasa and finally didn't burn my
mouth 🌶️🥲 #bali #bahasa #warung

---

## Hook (0–3s)
*(holding chopsticks, eyes wide, slightly comedic)*

> "Three days into Bali, I finally figured out how to say *no spicy* in
> Indonesian — because the smile-and-nod thing is not working."

**On-screen text:** *Day 3 of learning Bahasa for Bali*

## Setup (3–10s)
*(b-roll: previous attempt — tasting food, fanning mouth, comedic reaction)*

> "Saying *no spicy* in English just gets you a friendly nod and a meal that
> still sets you on fire."

## Teach (10–18s)
*(back to camera, repeats slowly)*

> "The phrase is *jangan pedas.* That's *don't* + *spicy.* I literally just
> stack them together when I order."

**On-screen text:** *jangan pedas = no spice*

## Situation (18–25s)
*(turns to the warung server, mimes ordering)*

> "*Saya mau nasi goreng, jangan pedas.* And — actually edible. Day 3, win."

**B-roll:** plate arrives, takes a happy bite.

## CTA (25–30s)
> "I'm drilling this stuff at speak-indo.com — 5 minutes, free. Link in bio."

**End card:** *Practice free → speak-indo.com*
