# Day 5 — AM — Foreign Learner — Villa check-in

**Archetype:** Foreign learner
**Length:** 25–30s
**Setting:** Walking into a villa lobby with a suitcase, golden hour light.
**Caption:** what to say in Bali when you check into your villa 🏝️ #bali
#villa #bahasa

---

## Hook (0–3s)
*(rolling a suitcase, looks up at camera)*

> "Day 5. Checking into a villa in Bali — and instead of saying *uh, hi,
> reservation,* I'm trying it in Indonesian."

**On-screen text:** *Day 5: villa check-in*

## Setup (3–8s)
*(quick comedic flashback — fumbling at a previous front desk)*

> "Last time I just kind of mumbled my last name and waved my passport. We
> can do better."

## Phrase (8–18s)
*(faces camera, lays it out)*

> "The phrase is: *Halo, saya check-in, atas nama [your name].* Hello, I'm
> checking in, under the name *[your name].*"

**On-screen text:**
- *halo = hello*
- *saya check-in = I'm checking in*
- *atas nama = under the name*

## Situation (18–25s)
*(walks up to the front desk, delivers it naturally)*

> "*Halo, saya check-in, atas nama Sam.* — and the staff lit up. They
> still spoke English back, but the vibe shifted. Day 5, win."

## CTA (25–30s)
> "I'm drilling these scenarios at speak-indo.com — 5 mins a day, free."

**End card:** *Practice free → speak-indo.com*

## Notes
- "Atas nama" is the magic phrase here — most foreigners don't know it. Lean
  into the *aha* moment in the caption: *"Atas nama" = under the name. Save
