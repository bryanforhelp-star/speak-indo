# Day 7 — PM — Indonesian Coach — 3 phrases that make locals smile

**Archetype:** Indonesian / local coach
**Length:** 28–35s
**Setting:** Bright background, or warung with locals subtly in the
background.
**Caption:** 3 Bahasa phrases that always make locals smile 🇮🇩 #bahasa #bali
#indonesia

---

## Hook (0–3s)
*(direct to camera, warm)*

> "Three phrases. If you use these in Bali, locals will treat you
> differently. Watch."

**On-screen text:** *3 phrases that make locals smile.*

## Phrase 1 — *terima kasih, kak* (3–11s)

> "One: *terima kasih, kak.* Not just *terima kasih.* Adding *kak* — it's
> the warm version. Like saying *thanks, friend.*"

**On-screen text:** ***terima kasih, kak*** *= thank you, friend*

## Phrase 2 — *enak sekali* (11–19s)

> "Two: *enak sekali.* Means *really delicious.* Say it after one bite at a
> warung. The cook will hear about it."

**On-screen text:** ***enak sekali*** *= really delicious*

## Phrase 3 — *sudah, terima kasih* (19–28s)

> "Three: *sudah, terima kasih.* Means *I'm full, thank you.* Use it when
> someone offers you more food. Polite, finished, grateful — all in three
> words."

**On-screen text:** ***sudah, terima kasih*** *= I'm full, thank you*
