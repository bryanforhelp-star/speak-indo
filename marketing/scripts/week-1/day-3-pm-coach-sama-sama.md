# Day 3 — PM — Indonesian Coach — *sama-sama*

**Archetype:** Indonesian / local coach
**Length:** 20–25s
**Setting:** Bright background or cafe counter. Daylight.
**Caption:** stop only saying *terima kasih* — here's what locals actually
reply with 🙏 #bahasa #bali #indonesia

---

## Hook (0–3s)
*(direct, friendly)*

> "Most foreigners learn one word in Indonesian — *terima kasih.* But what
> do you say when someone says it to you?"

**On-screen text:** *Someone says terima kasih to you. Now what?*

## Common mistake (3–8s)

> "Most people just smile awkwardly. Or say *thank you* back. Don't."

## Teach (8–18s)
*(slow, then natural)*

> "Reply with *sama-sama.* It literally means *same-same* — like *you're
> welcome*, *no problem*, *back at you* — all in one word."

**On-screen text:**
- *sama-sama = you're welcome*

## Situation (18–22s)
*(mimes a brief exchange)*

> "Someone holds the door — *terima kasih.* You reply — *sama-sama.* That's
> it. You sound like you live here."

## CTA (22–25s)
> "Practice it free at speak-indo.com."

**End card:** *Practice free → speak-indo.com*
