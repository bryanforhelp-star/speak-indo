# Day 2 — PM — Faceless — *saya di depan*

**Archetype:** Faceless Bali survival guide
**Length:** 18–22s
**Format:** AI VO + Bali b-roll + bold on-screen phrase + app screen rec at
end.
**Caption:** save this for Bali — when your Gojek driver calls and you can't
speak Indonesian 📞 #gojek #bali #bahasa

---

## Hook (0–3s)
**B-roll:** Gojek driver on a scooter pulling up, phone ringing on a hotel
nightstand.
**VO:** *"Save this if you're going to Bali."*
**On-screen text (large, bold):** *When your Gojek driver calls.*

## Problem (3–8s)
**B-roll:** foreigner staring at a ringing phone, confused face from a low
angle.
**VO:** *"They almost always call. They almost never speak English. You only
need one phrase."*

## Phrase (8–14s)
**B-roll:** zoom on a hotel entrance / villa gate.
**VO:** *"Saya di depan. It means: I'm out front."*
**On-screen text fills the frame:**
- *saya = I*
- *di depan = out front*
- ***saya di depan***

## Practice the sound (14–17s)
**VO:** *"Say it with me. Sa-ya di de-pan. Saya di depan."*

## CTA (17–22s)
**B-roll:** quick screen recording of the phrase being practiced inside the
Speak Indo app — user taps mic, says the phrase, gets a green checkmark.
**VO:** *"Drill it free at speak-indo.com."*
**End card:** *Practice free → speak-indo.com*
