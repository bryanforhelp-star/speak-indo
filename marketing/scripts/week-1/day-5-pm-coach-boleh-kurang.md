# Day 5 — PM — Indonesian Coach — *boleh kurang?*

**Archetype:** Indonesian / local coach
**Length:** 22–28s
**Setting:** Pasar / market backdrop, or a stall with sarongs, fruit, etc.
**Caption:** how to ask "can it be cheaper?" in Indonesian — politely 🛍️
#bahasa #bali #pasar

---

## Hook (0–3s)
*(direct to camera, friendly)*

> "Foreigners always ask me how to bargain in Bali. The trick is one polite
> phrase — not pressure."

**On-screen text:** *Bargaining in Bali, the polite way.*

## What not to do (3–8s)

> "Don't say *cheaper, cheaper, cheaper.* Don't push the price down hard.
> That's how you stop being welcome."

## Teach (8–18s)
*(smiling)*

> "Just ask: *boleh kurang?* It means *can it be less?* It's polite. Soft.
> The seller knows you mean *can we make a deal.*"

**On-screen text:**
- *boleh = can / may*
- *kurang = less*
- *boleh kurang? = can it be cheaper?*

## Situation (18–25s)
*(mimes picking up an item, raising eyebrows, smiling)*

> "Pick something up. Smile. *Boleh kurang?* If they say no — pay the
> price, smile, and say *terima kasih.* You did it right either way."

## CTA (25–28s)
> "Practice it free at speak-indo.com."

**End card:** *Practice free → speak-indo.com*
