## Hook (0–3s)

**On-screen text:** *5 words. Save this.*

## The 5 words (3–24s)

Each word: 3–4 seconds. B-roll cut + bold text fills frame + VO says the
word twice (slow, then natural).

1. **(3–7s) — *halo***
   - B-roll: warung greeting, smile.
   - VO: *"One. Halo. Hello. You'll use this every five minutes."*
   - On-screen: ***halo*** *= hello*

2. **(7–11s) — *terima kasih***
   - B-roll: handing over money, receiving a coffee.
   - VO: *"Two. Terima kasih. Thank you. Add 'kak' on the end and you sound
     like a local."*
   - On-screen: ***terima kasih*** *= thank you*

3. **(11–15s) — *jangan pedas***
   - B-roll: warung plate of food.
   - VO: *"Three. Jangan pedas. No spice. Trust us — say it."*
   - On-screen: ***jangan pedas*** *= no spice*

4. **(15–19s) — *boleh kurang***
   - B-roll: market stall, bag of fruit changing hands.
   - VO: *"Four. Boleh kurang. Can it be cheaper? Polite, not pushy."*
   - On-screen: ***boleh kurang*** *= can it be cheaper?*

5. **(19–24s) — *saya di depan***
   - B-roll: scooter pulling up, phone ringing.
   - VO: *"Five. Saya di depan. I'm out front. The Gojek phrase."*
   - On-screen: ***saya di depan*** *= I'm out front*
