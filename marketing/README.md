# Speak Indo Marketing

This folder is the operating system for Speak Indo's daily acquisition machine.

It's not a brand book. It's a working playbook — scripts, plans, outreach, and
metrics — used to ship 2 short-form videos a day, 3 SEO pages a week, and a
steady stream of partnership outreach.

## Positioning

> **Speak practical Indonesian for Bali life in 5 minutes a day.**

We do not compete with Duolingo on "learn Indonesian." We own:

- Practical Bahasa for **Bali**
- For **expats, tourists, digital nomads**
- For **daily life** — food, drivers, dating, villas, real conversations

Every piece of content, ad, and partner pitch should pass this test:

> Would a foreigner heading to Bali next month watch this and think
> *"oh, this is exactly what I need before Bali"*?

If not, it's the wrong angle.

## How to use it

- **Daily**: pull 2 scripts from `scripts/week-1/` (or week 2+ once written).
  Shoot, post to TikTok / IG Reels / YouTube Shorts. Log results in
  `metrics/north-star.md`.
- **Weekly**: ship 3 SEO pages from `seo/briefs.md`. Send 20-30 partner
  outreach DMs/emails from `partnerships/outreach-templates.md`. Review
  hook performance and double down on the top 3.
- **Monthly**: regenerate the next 30 days of scripts using the patterns in
  `scripts/hook-bank.md` and the winning hooks from the prior month.

## Content rules

1. **Outcome first, AI second.** Lead with "speak practical Indonesian for
   Bali life in 5 minutes a day." AI is the proof, not the pitch.
2. **One phrase per video.** Don't teach 5. Teach 1, well.
3. **Show the situation.** A warung, a Gojek call, checking into a villa —
   not a classroom.
4. **CTA is always practice, never download.** "Practice this free at
   speak-indo.com" beats "Download our app."
5. **Foreigners learn from Indonesians.** Never have a non-Indonesian creator
   pose as a native speaker. Learner POV is fine and relatable; "as a
   local…" is off-limits unless the creator is local.
